What you get, and how to apply the code

A Tesla referral code — also called a referral link, or a discount code for a new Tesla order — gives the buyer a bonus that Tesla sets per country and adjusts every few months. In Belgium the reward has taken the shape of a purchase discount, free Supercharging kilometres, or credits usable in the Tesla shop and app, depending on the campaign that is running. Because the amount changes, the honest answer is: check the number on Tesla's own referral page on the day you order.

  2. Check it before you pay the order fee

  3. Claim the reward after delivery

    Credits and Supercharging kilometres land in your Tesla account once the car is delivered and the order is finalised. A price discount is applied directly to the order total instead.

Referrals normally cover new vehicle orders. Used inventory, demo cars and some business or leasing constructions can be excluded — Tesla's referral terms page lists what currently applies.

Driving a Tesla in Belgium — my own experience

I drive my Tesla daily in Belgium: commuting, groceries, and the occasional long run down to France or over to the Netherlands. The short version is that after the first week the car stops being a gadget and simply becomes the most boring, reliable thing in the driveway — which is the highest compliment I can give a car.

Charging turned out to be the part I worried about most and think about least. Almost all of my charging happens overnight at home, so I leave every morning with a full battery. On longer trips the navigation plans the Supercharger stops itself, and the Belgian and Dutch networks are dense enough that a stop is a coffee, not a detour.

The things I appreciate most day to day are unglamorous: no fuel stops, very little maintenance beyond tyres and windscreen fluid, software updates that arrive over the air, and a cabin that is already warm when I get in. The things that took adjusting to: one-pedal driving for the first few days, and learning to read the range estimate rather than trust it blindly in winter.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Tesla referral code give me as a buyer?

Tesla sets the referral reward per country and changes it regularly. Depending on the running campaign it can be a discount on the purchase price, free Supercharging kilometres, or credits you can spend in the Tesla app. Check Tesla's official referral page for the amount that applies in Belgium on the day you order — this site cannot promise a specific figure.

How do I apply the referral code to my Tesla order?

Open the referral link before you configure your car, or paste the code into the referral field on the order page. The code has to be in place before you confirm and pay the order fee. Verify that the referral shows up in your order summary or in the Tesla app under your order details.

Can I add a referral code after I have already ordered?

No. Tesla only counts a referral that was attached at the moment the order was placed. Once the order is confirmed the referral field is locked, and support cannot add a code afterwards. If you have not ordered yet, use the link first.

Does using a referral code cost me anything?

No. The buyer pays exactly the same price for the car. The referral program is Tesla's own marketing scheme: the buyer receives the current bonus and the referrer receives credits from Tesla. There is no extra fee and no middleman.

Does the referral code work outside Belgium?

The code itself is not country-bound, but the reward is: Tesla runs the program per market, so what you receive in the Netherlands, France or Germany can differ from Belgium, and in some markets the program is paused entirely. Check Tesla's referral page for your delivery country before you order.
