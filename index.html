Driving a Tesla in Belgium — my own experience

I drive my Tesla daily in Belgium: commuting, groceries, and the occasional long run down to France or over to the Netherlands. The short version is that after the first week the car stops being a gadget and simply becomes the most boring, reliable thing in the driveway — which is the highest compliment I can give a car.

Charging turned out to be the part I worried about most and think about least. Almost all of my charging happens overnight at home, so I leave every morning with a full battery. On longer trips the navigation plans the Supercharger stops itself, and the Belgian and Dutch networks are dense enough that a stop is a coffee, not a detour. Winter costs range — cold weather and motorway speeds eat into it noticeably — so I plan a little more margin between November and March.

The things I appreciate most day to day are unglamorous: no fuel stops, very little maintenance beyond tyres and windscreen fluid, software updates that arrive over the air, and a cabin that is already warm when I get in. The things that took adjusting to: one-pedal driving for the first few days, and learning to read the range estimate rather than trust it blindly in winter.

Frequently asked questions

What does the referral code give me as a buyer?

Tesla sets the reward per country and changes it regularly. Depending on the running campaign it can be a discount on the purchase price, free Supercharging kilometres, or credits you can spend in the Tesla app. Check Tesla's official referral page for the amount that applies in Belgium on the day you order — this site cannot promise a specific figure.

How do I apply the referral code to my order?

Open the referral link before you configure your car, or paste the code into the referral field on the order page. The code has to be in place before you confirm and pay the order fee. Verify that the referral appears in your order summary or in the Tesla app.

Can I add a referral code after I have already ordered?

No. Tesla only counts a referral that was attached at the moment the order was placed. Once the order is confirmed the referral field is locked and support cannot add a code afterwards. If you have not ordered yet, use the link first.

Does using a referral code cost me anything?

No. You pay exactly the same price for the car. The referral program is Tesla's own marketing scheme: you receive the current bonus, and I receive credits from Tesla. There is no extra fee and no middleman.

Does the code work for used and demo Teslas?

Usually not. Referrals normally apply to new vehicle orders only, and Tesla can exclude used inventory, demo cars, business leases and certain promotions. Tesla's referral terms page lists the current exclusions for your country.
